I am an MSc student in Animal Sciences at Wageningen University &amp; Research, with a background in aquaculture and veterinary practice. I completed my BSc in Aquaculture at China Agricultural University and a minor degree in Comparative Law. I am also a veterinary practitioner qualified in China.

My previous research spans aquatic animal behaviour, enzymatic hydrolysis and process optimisation, and field surveys with the Chinese Academy of Sciences. I am interested in animal genetics and breeding, especially how quantitative and image-based methods can support phenotyping and animal production research.

## Research interests

- Animal genetics and breeding
- Quantitative and image-based phenotyping
- Computer vision for animal production research
- Aquaculture and animal behaviour
