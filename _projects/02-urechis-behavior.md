This undergraduate research project examined how temperature relates to growth, behaviour, and water quality for *Urechis unicinctus*. I contributed as a research assistant at China Agricultural University's Yantai Institute.

- Worked with behavioural observations and image-recognition methods.
- Considered animal responses alongside environmental measurements.
