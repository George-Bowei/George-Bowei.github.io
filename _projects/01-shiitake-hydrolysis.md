As project leader, I studied the preparation and functional properties of hydrolysate from *Lentinus edodes* (shiitake mushroom).

- Used response surface methodology to optimise the manufacturing process.
- Evaluated hydrolysis products by quantitative determination of free amino acids.
- Published the study in *Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems* in 2025.
