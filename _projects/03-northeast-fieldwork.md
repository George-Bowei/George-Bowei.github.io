As a field expedition member with the Chinese Academy of Sciences, I joined biodiversity and pasture-resource surveys across eight cities in northeastern China.

- Collected plant specimens and soil from 80 sites.
- Collected and preserved more than 1,000 botanical specimens following standard protocols.
- Surveyed grass and pasture resources and contributed to a regional database.
- Used GPS and GIS tools for habitat mapping and field data collection.
