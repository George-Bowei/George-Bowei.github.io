01 / A SHORT INTRODUCTION

Biology first.
Methods that make it visible.

I am an MSc student in Animal Sciences at Wageningen University & Research, with a background in aquaculture and veterinary practice. My studies bring together animal biology, research methods, and a growing interest in genetics and breeding.

Before Wageningen, I studied Aquaculture at China Agricultural University and completed a minor in Law. My research experience spans aquatic animal behaviour, enzymatic hydrolysis and process optimisation, and field surveys in northeastern China.

I am especially interested in quantitative genetics, animal breeding, and computer vision—and in how quantitative and image-based approaches can support phenotyping and animal production research.
